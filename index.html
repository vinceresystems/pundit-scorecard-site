Standings

Sorted by profit. The big number is how often the pick covered the spread. Anything over 52.4% makes money at standard odds.

How to read this

Against the spread

The pick wins if the team beats the point spread, not just the game. Chiefs −3 needs the Chiefs to win by 4 or more.

Closing line

The spread at kickoff, from nflverse. Every pick is graded against it so all three are held to the same number. Where a pundit quoted his own line on air, that record is kept too.

Units

Profit if you bet 1.1 to win 1 on every pick, the standard −110 price. A win is +1, a loss is −1.1, a push is 0. That is why 52.4% is breakeven.

Picked the winner

How often the team he picked simply won the game, ignoring the spread.
